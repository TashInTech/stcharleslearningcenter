About us

More about our school

We are a private mixed day primary school that believes in creating opportunities and empowering young people through education. St.Charles Learning Center was established in 2022 with a goal of providing basic primary education to needy children in Kamarya Village, Kiruhura District, in Western Uganda. The school is registered under the Uganda Ministry of Education and Sports.

St.Charles Learning Center currently has an enrollment of 102 children — 52 girls and 50 boys — supported by 22 teachers and staff. Lessons are taught in a temporary wooden shelter while a permanent classroom block is under construction to give pupils a safe, weather-resistant space to learn.

Our flagship ongoing project is the Classroom Block Construction Project — replacing the wooden shelter that floods during rains with a permanent block that will also serve as a venue for community training and youth empowerment workshops.

Ongoing project

The Classroom Block Construction Project

St.Charles Learning Center was established in 2022 to provide basic education to needy and poor children in Kamarya Village. The school currently has an enrollment of 102 children — 52 girls and 50 boys — but is facing a shortage of classroom space for daily lessons. Children currently study under a shelter made of wood and timber; when it rains, water enters the shelter and disrupts lessons and damages learning aids.

It is on this background that we are requesting your charitable support to help construct a new classroom block and create a decent learning environment for our children.
